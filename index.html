AI Shorts Factory is a Windows desktop application that helps an authorized creator research topics, generate original educational scripts, render vertical videos, and upload reviewed content to connected social platforms.

How it works Read the Privacy Policy

About the application

The application is designed for technology, science, engineering, artificial intelligence, space, and educational explainer content. It runs locally on the creator's Windows computer and stores project files, rendered media, authorization tokens, and upload history on that computer.

AI Shorts Factory does not operate a public social network, sell user profiles, or provide access to unrelated third parties. It connects only to services that the creator explicitly authorizes.

Core safeguards

  • Human review is required before platform upload.
  • TikTok uploads are sent as drafts for final review and posting.
  • YouTube uploads can remain private until the creator publishes them.
  • Duplicate-upload protection is maintained in local history.
  • Authorization can be revoked through each connected platform.

How the workflow operates

1

Research and generation

The creator starts a local run. The application gathers permitted trend signals and generates an original plan, narration, metadata, and visual instructions.

2

Local rendering

The application renders a vertical MP4 and saves the plan and media in a local run folder on the creator's computer.

3

Human review

The creator reviews the narration, captions, visuals, metadata, sources, and platform settings before approving any upload.

4

Platform delivery

After approval, the application uploads the existing reviewed MP4. TikTok content is delivered as a draft so the creator can complete final settings and post it.

Data practices

The public website is informational and does not include account registration, advertising trackers, or a contact form. The desktop application processes only the information needed to create content, authorize connected services, upload approved media, and maintain local operational history.

Additional details—including categories of data, service providers, retention, deletion, and user choices—are described in the Privacy Policy.

Connected services

Depending on the creator's configuration, the application may interact with OpenAI, Google/YouTube, and TikTok through their official APIs. Each service has its own terms and privacy practices.

AI Shorts Factory is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok, YouTube, Google, OpenAI, GitHub, or Microsoft.
