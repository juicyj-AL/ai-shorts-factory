Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Shorts Factory ("the Application," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when an authorized creator uses the locally installed desktop application or visits this informational website.

1. Scope

AI Shorts Factory is a desktop content-production workflow operated by its authorized creator. The Application helps research topics, generate original short-form video plans, render media, and upload creator-approved content to connected platforms. This website provides public information about the Application and its policies.

2. Information processed by the desktop application

The Application may process the following categories of information:

  • Authorization information: OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, platform account identifiers, and granted scopes needed to connect to services such as YouTube and TikTok.
  • Content information: topics, scripts, titles, descriptions, tags, captions, source references, visual prompts, audio, images, and rendered video files.
  • Operational records: local run identifiers, timestamps, file paths, upload status, platform publish identifiers, error messages, and basic performance statistics returned by connected platforms.
  • Configuration information: application preferences and API credentials supplied by the authorized creator.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • authenticate the creator with services the creator chooses to connect;
  • research, generate, render, review, and deliver original short-form content;
  • prevent accidental duplicate uploads and maintain local operational history;
  • refresh platform authorization and retrieve permitted upload or performance status;
  • diagnose errors, maintain security, and improve the creator's local workflow.

4. Human review and publishing

The workflow is designed to require creator review before upload. TikTok uploads are delivered as drafts for the creator to review and post within TikTok. YouTube uploads may be configured as private until the creator changes their visibility.

5. Storage and retention

Project media, local history, configuration, and OAuth token files are stored on the creator's computer unless a connected service receives information as part of an authorized API request. Local files remain until the creator deletes them, removes the application, or follows a configured retention process.

6. Service providers and platform APIs

Depending on configuration, the Application may transmit information to OpenAI, Google/YouTube, and TikTok through their official APIs. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not sell personal information or provide it to data brokers.

7. Website data

This website does not provide user accounts, advertising, analytics code, or a contact form. It is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information necessary to operate and secure its hosting service under GitHub's own privacy practices.

8. Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, including local token storage, restricted credential files, OAuth authorization, explicit approval controls, and duplicate-upload protection. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Choices, access, and deletion

The authorized creator can stop processing by closing or removing the Application, delete local project and token files, and revoke platform access through the relevant YouTube/Google or TikTok account settings. Platform-hosted content must be managed through the applicable platform.

10. Children's privacy

The Application is intended for use by an adult creator and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly operate a service that collects personal information from children.

11. International processing

Connected providers may process information in countries other than the creator's location. Their policies describe applicable transfer and storage practices.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Application, connected services, or legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
